# Mystery Cards & Magic Bag

### Objective

Practice **What is this?** and **What is in your bag?**

Use **a** and **an** to talk about one thing.

---

### Materials

Prepare picture cards:

- tree
- rabbit
- apple
- elephant
- orange
- person

Prepare one **mystery bag** (a backpack or small bag).

---

### Game 1 — Mystery Card

1) Pick a card.

A student takes one card and hides it.

2) Show slowly.

The student shows the card little by little.

**What is this?**

A student answers:

**This is a / an ______.**

Examples:

**This is a tree.**

**This is a rabbit.**

**This is an apple.**

**This is an elephant.**

**This is an orange.**

**This is a person.**

If correct → the student keeps the card.

---

### Game 2 — Magic Bag

Put some cards inside the bag.

One student closes their eyes and takes one card.

**What is in your bag?**

The student looks and answers:

**A / An ______.**

Examples:

**A tree.**

**A rabbit.**

**An apple.**

**An elephant.**

---

### Speed Round

Show cards very quickly.

Students must answer fast:

**This is a tree!**

**This is an apple!**

**This is an elephant!**

Fast and correct answers score points.

---

### Action Round

Students act like the object.

Example:

A student acts like an elephant.

**What is this?**

Students answer:

**This is an elephant!**

---

### Mini Challenge

Students open their bag.

**What is in your bag?**

Students answer:

**A / An ______.**

---

### Finish

**What is this?**

**This is a / an ______.**

**What is in your bag?**

**A / An ______.**
