# God’s Good World

### Objective

Help students understand three simple truths:

**God is the creator.**

**Everything God makes is good.**

**God loves people.**

---

### Materials

Prepare picture cards or drawings:

- light
- sky
- water
- land
- trees
- sun
- moon
- stars
- animals
- people

Prepare one large paper or poster for the class.

---

### Step 1 — Look at the World

The teacher shows a picture.

**Look!**

The teacher asks:

**What is this?**

Students answer:

**This is the ______.**

Examples:

**This is the sun.**

**This is the sky.**

**This is the water.**

**This is the land.**

---

### Step 2 — Build the World

Put a large paper on the board.

**The World**

Students come to the front and place pictures on the poster.

Each student says:

**This is the ______.**

Examples:

**This is the sun.**

**This is the moon.**

**This is the water.**

**This is the land.**

**These are trees.**

---

### Step 3 — God Is Good

When the poster is ready, the teacher points to the world.

The teacher says:

**God is the creator.**

Students repeat:

**God is the creator.**

The teacher says:

**God is good.**

Students repeat:

**God is good.**

---

### Step 4 — People

The teacher shows the picture of people.

The teacher asks:

**What is this?**

Students answer:

**This is a person.**

The teacher says:

**God loves people.**

Students repeat:

**God loves people.**

---

### Creative Moment

Students draw something from the world.

Examples:

- the sun
- the sky
- trees
- animals
- people

Students color their drawings.

---

### Show and Share

Students show their drawing.

The class asks:

**What is this?**

The student answers:

**This is the ______.**

---

### Closing Message

**God is the creator.**

**God is good.**

**God loves you.**
